# B1.6 – Writing: CV & Job Email

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Understand the basic structure of a simple CV
- Write a short and clear job application email
- Use polite and professional language in writing

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Discuss the questions below:

1. Have you ever written a CV or job application email?
2. What information should be included in a CV?
3. Why is a good job email important?

👉 Speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### A Simple CV (Example)

**Name:** Nguyen Minh Anh  
**Email:** minhanh@email.com  
**Phone:** 09xxxxxxxx  

**Profile**  
A motivated university student looking for a part-time office position.

**Education**  
- Bachelor of Business Administration (2022 – present)

**Work Experience**  
- Part-time Office Assistant (2023)  
  - Answered emails and supported customers  
  - Prepared simple reports  

**Skills**  
- Good communication skills  
- Basic computer skills  
- Teamwork

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. What position is Minh Anh looking for?  
2. What experience does she have?  
3. Which skills are mentioned?

---

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Useful CV Language

- looking for a position as …
- responsible for …
- experience in …
- good communication skills
- able to work in a team

---

### 2️⃣ Polite Language for Job Emails

- Dear Sir or Madam,
- I am writing to apply for the position of …
- I would like to introduce myself.
- I believe I am suitable for this position.
- Thank you for your time and consideration.
- Kind regards / Best regards,

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task 1: CV (Short)

### Task
Write a **simple CV** (about **80–100 words**).

Include:
- personal information (name, contact)
- education
- work experience (if any)
- skills

🔔 **Tip:**  
Keep sentences short and clear. Use bullet points if possible.

## ✉️ Part 5 – Writing Task 2: Job Application Email

### Task
Write a **job application email** (80–120 words).

You should write:
- why you are writing
- what position you are applying for
- your experience and skills
- a polite closing

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1:**  
- Reason for writing  
- Position you are applying for  

**Paragraph 2:**  
- Your experience and skills  
- Why you are suitable  

**Paragraph 3:**  
- Polite ending  

---

### 📄 Sample Job Email (Optional)

<details>
<summary><strong>Show / Hide Sample Job Email</strong></summary>

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to apply for the position of office assistant at your company.  
I am currently a university student and I have experience working part-time in an office, where I answered emails and helped customers.

I have good communication skills and I can work well in a team.  
I believe I am suitable for this position and would like to learn more in a professional environment.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Kind regards,  
Tran Hoang Nam

</details>

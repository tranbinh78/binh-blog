## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about family
- Practice grammar in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Lan lives with her parents and her younger brother.  
Her father works in an office, and her mother is a teacher.  
Lan is very close to her family.  
They often have dinner together in the evening.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. Who does Lan live with?  
2. What does her father do?  
3. When does the family have dinner together?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. This is ___ mother.  
a) my  
b) I  

2. Her parents ___ dinner together.  
a) has  
b) have  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete about your family:

- I live with ___.
- My father / mother works as ___.
- We often ___ together.

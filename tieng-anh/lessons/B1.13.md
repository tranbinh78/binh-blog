# B1.13 – Healthy Lifestyle

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Living a Healthy Life

A healthy lifestyle is important for people of all ages. It helps us feel better, have more energy, and reduce the risk of illness. Many people believe that living healthily is difficult, but small changes can make a big difference.

Regular exercise is one of the most important habits for good health. People who exercise often have stronger bodies and feel less stressed. In addition, eating balanced meals with vegetables, fruit, and enough water helps the body work properly.

Another key part of a healthy lifestyle is getting enough sleep. When people sleep well, they can concentrate better and feel more positive during the day. On the other hand, unhealthy habits such as eating too much fast food or sitting for a long time can cause health problems.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Health & Lifestyle

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| lifestyle | the way a person lives |
| exercise | physical activity to stay healthy |
| balanced diet | eating different types of healthy food |
| stress | mental or physical pressure |
| energetic | full of energy |
| habit | something you do regularly |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- Regular exercise is good for your health.
- I try to eat a balanced diet.
- Getting enough sleep helps me feel better.
- Unhealthy habits can cause health problems.

👉 Make **3 sentences** about your own lifestyle.

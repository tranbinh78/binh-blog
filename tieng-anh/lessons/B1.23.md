## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Causes and Solutions to Environmental Problems

Many environmental problems are caused by human activities. For example, air pollution is often caused by traffic and factories that release harmful gases. Water pollution happens when waste is thrown into rivers and lakes, and plastic waste increases because people use too many single-use products.

However, these problems can be reduced if people take action. One important solution is recycling. When people recycle paper, plastic, and metal, less waste goes into the environment. Another solution is using public transport or bicycles instead of private cars. This helps reduce air pollution and saves energy.

Governments and individuals both play an important role. If governments create stricter environmental laws and people change daily habits, environmental problems can be reduced over time.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Talking about Causes

- … is caused by …
- One reason for … is …
- … happens because …

Examples:
- Air pollution is caused by traffic.
- One reason for plastic waste is the use of single-use bags.

---

### 2️⃣ Talking about Solutions

- One solution is to …
- People should …
- Governments can …

Examples:
- One solution is to recycle more.
- People should save energy at home.

---

### 3️⃣ Linking Cause and Effect

- because
- so
- as a result
- therefore

Examples:
- People use too much plastic, **so** pollution increases.
- Factories release waste; **as a result**, water becomes polluted.

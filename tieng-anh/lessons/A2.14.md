## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about shopping for food
- Practice grammar in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Hoa goes to the market every morning.  
She buys vegetables, fruit, and some meat.  
She also buys bread and milk.  
She likes fresh food.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. Where does Hoa go every morning?  
2. What food does she buy?  
3. Does she like fresh food?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. She buys ___ vegetables.  
a) a  
b) some  

2. She buys ___ apple.  
a) an  
b) some  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete:
- I usually buy ___ and ___.
- I drink ___ every day.

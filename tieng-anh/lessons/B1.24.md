## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Paragraph)

### Plastic Waste in Cities

Plastic waste is a serious problem in many cities today.  
People use plastic bags and bottles every day, but a large amount of this waste is not recycled. As a result, plastic pollutes rivers, streets, and oceans, and it harms animals and the environment.

One important solution is to reduce the use of single-use plastic. People should use reusable bags and bottles instead. In addition, governments can encourage recycling by providing more recycling bins and educating the public.

If individuals and governments work together, plastic waste can be reduced and cities can become cleaner and healthier.

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Structure of a Problem–Solution Paragraph

A good problem–solution paragraph usually includes **3 parts**:

1. **Problem**  
   - Describe the problem clearly  
   - Example: *Plastic waste is a serious environmental problem.*

2. **Causes / Effects**  
   - Explain why the problem happens or its effects  
   - Example: *People use too much single-use plastic.*

3. **Solutions**  
   - Suggest 1–2 practical solutions  
   - Example: *People should use reusable bags.*

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Linking Words

- Cause: **because, since**
- Result: **as a result, therefore**
- Solutions: **one solution is, another solution is**
- Condition: **if**

Examples:
- People use too much plastic; **as a result**, pollution increases.
- **One solution is** to recycle more.
- **If** people change their habits, the problem can be reduced.

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (120–150 words)

### Topic: An Environmental Problem and Its Solutions

Write a **problem–solution paragraph** about ONE topic:

- Air pollution in cities  
- Plastic waste  
- Water pollution  

You should:
- describe the problem
- explain its causes or effects
- suggest **1–2 solutions**
- use linking words

🔔 **Tip:**  
Write in a **neutral style**.  
Focus on solutions that are **realistic and simple**.

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Sentence 1–2:**  
- Introduce the problem

**Sentence 3–4:**  
- Explain causes or effects

**Sentence 5–7:**  
- Suggest solutions

**Final sentence:**  
- Give a hopeful conclusion

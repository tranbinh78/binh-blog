- Understand a short conversation about work and study
- Ask and answer questions about jobs and classes

### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. What does Anna do?  
a) She is a student  
b) She works in an office  

2. What does she study / do after work?  
a) Studies English  
b) Watches TV  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. Anna works in an ___.  
2. She studies ___ in the evening.

## 💬 Part 2 – Dialogue Practice

Practice the conversation with a partner.

---

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking Practice

Answer:
1. What do you do? (work / study)
2. Where do you work or study?

---

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task

Talk about your work or studies (5–6 sentences).  
Use:
- Present Simple
- adverbs of frequency

✔ Understand work/study conversations

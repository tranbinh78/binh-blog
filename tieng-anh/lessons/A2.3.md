### 🔊 Listening Task 1: Listen and choose

Listen to the conversation and choose the correct answer.

1. What time does Nam wake up?  
a) 5:30  
b) 6:00  
c) 6:30  

2. What does Nam do in the evening?  
a) Goes out  
b) Watches TV  
c) Studies English  

---

### 🔊 Listening Script
<details>
<summary><strong>Show / Hide Listening Script</strong></summary>

Nam: Hi Lan. What time do you usually wake up?  
Lan: I usually wake up at six. How about you?  
Nam: I wake up at six thirty.  
Lan: What do you do in the evening?  
Nam: I often watch TV and sometimes study English.

</details>

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Listening Task 2: Complete

Listen again and complete the sentences.

1. Lan usually wakes up at ___.  
2. Nam wakes up at ___.  
3. Nam sometimes studies ___ in the evening.

## 💬 Part 3 – Dialogue Practice

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Practice

Ask and answer:

- What time do you usually wake up?
- What do you do in the evening?

## 🗣️ Part 5 – Speaking Task

Talk about your daily routine (5–6 sentences).  
Use:
- time expressions
- adverbs of frequency

## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Ask and answer about daily routines
- Speak about time and habits

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Useful Questions

- What time do you wake up?
- What do you do in the morning?
- What time do you go to bed?

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Model Dialogue

A: What time do you usually wake up?  
B: I usually wake up at six.  
A: What do you do in the evening?  
B: I often relax and watch TV.

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Speaking Practice

Answer in full sentences:

1. What time do you wake up?  
2. What do you do after work or school?  
3. What do you do in the evening?

---

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task

Talk about your daily routine (5–6 sentences).  
Use:
- time (morning / evening)
- adverbs (usually / often / never)

---

## ✅ What you can do now
✔ Ask about routines  
✔ Talk about your daily life clearly

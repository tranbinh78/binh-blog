## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about daily routine
- Practice grammar in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Hoa is a student.  
She usually wakes up at 6 a.m. and goes to school at 7.  
She has lunch at school.  
In the evening, she often does her homework and relaxes.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. What time does Hoa wake up?  
2. Where does she have lunch?  
3. What does she do in the evening?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. Hoa ___ wakes up at six.  
a) usually  
b) never  

2. She ___ to school at seven.  
a) go  
b) goes  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete about **you**:

- I wake up at ___.
- I go to work / school at ___.
- In the evening, I usually ___.

---

✔ Understand a routine text

## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Learn basic vocabulary about jobs and study
- Use Present Simple to talk about work and study

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Vocabulary: Work & Study

| Word | Meaning |
|----|----|
| job | công việc |
| work | làm việc |
| study | học |
| office | văn phòng |
| company | công ty |
| student | học sinh / sinh viên |
| teacher | giáo viên |
| class | lớp học |

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Present Simple (Work & Study)

### Form
- I work in an office.
- She studies English.

### Negative
- I don’t work on Sunday.
- He doesn’t study in the evening.

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Adverbs of Frequency (Review)

always – usually – often – sometimes – never  

Examples:
- I usually start work at eight.
- She often studies in the evening.

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Guided Practice

Complete:
1. I work / study at ___.
2. I usually ___ in the morning.
3. I don’t ___ on Sunday.

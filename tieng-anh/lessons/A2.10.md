## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about hobbies
- Practice grammar in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Nam has a lot of free time on weekends.  
He likes playing football and watching TV.  
He doesn’t like staying at home all day.  
In the evening, he often listens to music.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. When does Nam have free time?  
2. What does he like doing?  
3. What doesn’t he like?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. Nam ___ playing football.  
a) like  
b) likes  

2. He doesn’t like ___ at home.  
a) stay  
b) staying  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete about you:

- In my free time, I like ___.
- I don’t like ___.
- In the evening, I often ___.

---

## ✅ What you can do now
✔ Understand a text about hobbies  
✔ Use likes and dislikes correctly

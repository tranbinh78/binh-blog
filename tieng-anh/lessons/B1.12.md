# B1.12 – Writing: For and Against Essay (Short)

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a short **for and against essay** (120–150 words)
- Present **both advantages and disadvantages** clearly
- Use **linking words** to organize ideas logically
- Give a short, balanced conclusion

Discuss the questions below:

1. Do you think technology makes life better or worse?
2. Is social media more helpful than harmful?
3. Should students use smartphones in class?

👉 Try to give **one advantage and one disadvantage** for each question.

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Essay)

### Is Technology Helpful or Harmful?

Technology has become an important part of modern life, and it brings both advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, technology makes daily activities easier and faster. People can communicate instantly, work online, and access information quickly. In education, students can learn at their own pace using online platforms and digital resources.

On the other hand, technology can also cause problems. Spending too much time on screens may affect health and reduce face-to-face communication. In addition, people may become too dependent on digital devices.

In conclusion, although technology has some disadvantages, I believe its benefits are greater if it is used in a balanced way.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. What advantages of technology are mentioned?
2. What problems does technology cause?
3. What is the writer’s final opinion?

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Structure of a For and Against Essay

A short for and against essay usually has **4 parts**:

1. **Introduction**  
   - Introduce the topic  
   - Example: *Technology has become an important part of modern life.*

2. **Advantages paragraph**  
   - Talk about benefits  
   - Use linking words: *on the one hand, first, moreover*

3. **Disadvantages paragraph**  
   - Talk about problems  
   - Use linking words: *on the other hand, however*

4. **Conclusion**  
   - Give a balanced opinion or personal view  
   - Example: *In conclusion, technology is useful if used carefully.*

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Linking Words

- Introducing sides: **on the one hand / on the other hand**
- Adding ideas: **moreover, also**
- Contrasting: **however**
- Concluding: **in conclusion, overall**

Examples:
- On the one hand, online learning is convenient.  
- However, it can reduce interaction.  
- In conclusion, a balance is necessary.

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (120–150 words)

### Topic: Technology in Modern Life

Write a **for and against essay** about the topic.

You should:
- introduce the topic
- discuss **advantages**
- discuss **disadvantages**
- give a short conclusion

🔔 **Tip:**  
Do not give your personal opinion too early.  
Save it for the **conclusion**.

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1 – Introduction:**  
- Introduce the topic clearly

**Paragraph 2 – Advantages:**  
- Give 2 advantages with examples

**Paragraph 3 – Disadvantages:**  
- Give 2 disadvantages with examples

**Paragraph 4 – Conclusion:**  
- Give a balanced or personal opinion

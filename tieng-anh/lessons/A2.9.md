## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Learn vocabulary for free time activities
- Use like / love / don’t like / hate

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Vocabulary: Free Time Activities

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| free time | thời gian rảnh |
| hobby | sở thích |
| read books | đọc sách |
| listen to music | nghe nhạc |
| watch TV | xem TV |
| play sports | chơi thể thao |
| go out | đi chơi |
| stay at home | ở nhà |

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Likes and Dislikes

### Structure
- I like reading.
- I love music.
- I don’t like watching TV.
- I hate getting up early.

### Questions
- What do you like doing?
- Do you like sports?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Guided Examples

- I like ___ in my free time.
- I don’t like ___.
- My hobby is ___.

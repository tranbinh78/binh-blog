## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Countable & Uncountable

### Countable
- an apple / two apples

### Uncountable
- rice, water, milk

Use:
- **a / an** (countable)
- **some / any** (uncountable)

Examples:
- I eat **an apple**.
- I drink **some water**.

# B1.25 – Future Plans and Ambitions

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about future plans and ambitions

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Planning for the Future

Many people think about their future plans as they grow older. Some focus on their education, while others plan to build a successful career or improve their lifestyle. Having clear goals helps people stay motivated and make better decisions.

For example, students often plan to finish their studies and find a suitable job. To achieve this, they may learn new skills, improve their language ability, or gain work experience. Other people plan to travel, start a business, or support their families.

Although the future is uncertain, planning ahead can help people feel more confident. Even small plans can make a big difference if people work hard and stay focused.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Talking About the Future

**Going to** – plans and intentions  
- I am going to find a job after graduation.
- She is going to study abroad.

**Will** – decisions, hopes, predictions  
- I will try my best to achieve my goals.
- I think technology will change many jobs.

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Vocabulary: Plans & Goals

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| plan | something you decide to do in the future |
| goal | something you want to achieve |
| ambition | a strong desire to succeed |
| career | a long-term job path |
| opportunity | a good chance to do something |
| prepare | to get ready for the future |

---

### 3️⃣ Useful Sentences

- I am going to focus on my studies.
- My goal is to find a stable job.
- I hope to improve my skills in the future.
- Planning ahead helps me feel confident.

👉 Make **3 sentences** about your own future plans.

## ✍️ Part 5 – Short Writing (80–100 words)

### Topic: My Future Plans

Write about:
- what you plan to do in the future
- your goals or ambitions
- how you plan to achieve them

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use **going to** for plans and **will** for hopes or predictions.

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Agreeing and Disagreeing in Daily Life

People often have different opinions, especially when discussing work, study, or daily decisions. Agreeing and disagreeing politely helps conversations remain positive and respectful.

When people agree, they show support and understanding. Simple expressions such as *“I agree with you”* or *“That’s a good point”* help build trust. However, disagreement is also natural. Instead of saying *“You are wrong”*, people can use softer language like *“I see your point, but I think…”*.

Using polite expressions allows people to share ideas without causing conflict. As a result, discussions become more productive, and relationships become stronger.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Expressions for Agreeing

- I agree with you.
- That’s a good idea.
- I think so too.
- Exactly.

Examples:
- I agree with you. Teamwork is very important.
- That’s a good idea. We should try it.

---

### 2️⃣ Expressions for Disagreeing Politely

- I see your point, but …
- I’m not sure about that.
- I agree to some extent, but …
- That may be true, however …

Examples:
- I see your point, but working online is more convenient.
- I agree to some extent, but we need more time.

---

### 3️⃣ Giving Reasons

- because …
- since …
- the reason is that …

Examples:
- I disagree because it is too expensive.
- I think this way since it saves time.

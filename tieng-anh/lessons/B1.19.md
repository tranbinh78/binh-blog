## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### The Importance of Communication

Good communication is the key to healthy relationships. Whether it is between family members, friends, or colleagues, clear communication helps people understand each other better and avoid misunderstandings.

In families, communication helps members share feelings and support one another. Friends who communicate openly can solve problems more easily and build trust. At work, effective communication is important for teamwork and productivity. People need to listen carefully, express ideas clearly, and respect different opinions.

However, communication problems can still happen. Sometimes people do not listen carefully, or they are afraid to express their true feelings. Using polite language and choosing the right time to talk can help improve communication and strengthen relationships.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Relationships & Communication

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| relationship | the way people are connected |
| communication | sharing information or feelings |
| trust | belief that someone is honest |
| misunderstanding | a wrong idea or belief |
| express feelings | say how you feel |
| respect | show politeness and care |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- Good communication builds trust.
- It’s important to listen carefully.
- People should express their feelings politely.
- Misunderstandings can damage relationships.

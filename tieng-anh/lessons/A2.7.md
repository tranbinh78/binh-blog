### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. Who does Minh live with?  
a) His parents  
b) His friends  
c) His wife  

2. Who is friendly?  
a) Minh’s father  
b) Minh’s sister  
c) Minh’s friend  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. Minh lives with his ___.
2. His sister is very ___.

## 💬 Part 2 – Dialogue Practice

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking Practice

Answer:
1. Who do you live with?
2. What is your family member like?

---

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task

Talk about your family (5–6 sentences).  
Use:
- family words
- possessive adjectives
- simple adjectives

## 🧠 Part 2 – Grammar Review

### 1️⃣ Verb Tenses Review

**Present Simple**
- habits, facts  
- Example: I work in an office.

**Past Simple**
- finished past events  
- Example: I travelled to Hue last year.

**Present Perfect**
- experiences, results  
- Example: I have worked here for two years.

**Future Forms**
- **Going to**: plans  
- **Will**: predictions, decisions  
- Example: I am going to apply for a new job.

---

### 2️⃣ Conditionals (First Conditional)

**Form:**  
If + present simple, will / may / might + base verb

Examples:
- If I study hard, I will pass the exam.
- If people recycle more, pollution may decrease.

---

### 3️⃣ Modals & Advice

- **should / shouldn’t** – advice  
- **might / may** – possibility  

Examples:
- You should exercise regularly.
- Technology might change many jobs.

---

### 4️⃣ Agreeing & Disagreeing

- I agree with you.
- I see your point, but …
- That may be true; however, …

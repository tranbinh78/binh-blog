## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about health problems
- Practice advice language in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Lan is not feeling well today.  
She has a headache and feels very tired.  
She goes to bed early and drinks a lot of water.  
She doesn’t go to work today.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. How does Lan feel?  
2. What health problem does she have?  
3. What does she do to feel better?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. She ___ go to work today.  
a) should  
b) shouldn’t  

2. She ___ drink a lot of water.  
a) should  
b) shouldn’t  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete:
- I feel ___ today.
- I have a ___.
- I should ___.

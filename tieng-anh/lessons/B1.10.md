# B1.10 – Technology in Daily Life

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about how technology is used in daily life
- Understand common benefits and problems of technology
- Use key vocabulary related to technology and media

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. What technology do you use every day?
2. How has technology changed the way you study or work?
3. Can you think of one problem caused by technology?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Technology Around Us

Technology plays an important role in modern life. Many people use smartphones, computers, and the internet every day to communicate, work, and relax. Thanks to technology, people can send messages instantly, attend online meetings, and access information easily.

In education, technology helps students learn more effectively. Online platforms allow learners to watch lessons, practise skills, and review materials at their own pace. In addition, teachers can use digital tools to make lessons more interesting and interactive.

However, technology also has some disadvantages. Spending too much time on screens can cause health problems such as eye strain and poor sleep. Moreover, people may become less active or depend too much on technology in their daily lives. For this reason, it is important to use technology in a balanced way.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Technology & Media

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| device | a piece of electronic equipment |
| access | the ability to use something |
| platform | an online system or website |
| digital | related to computers and the internet |
| screen time | time spent using electronic screens |
| depend on | to need or rely on something |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- Technology helps people communicate more easily.
- I use my smartphone to access information quickly.
- Too much screen time can cause health problems.
- It is important to use technology in a balanced way.

👉 Make **3 sentences** about your own use of technology.

## ✍️ Part 5 – Short Writing (80–100 words)

### Topic: Technology in My Daily Life

Write about:
- what technology you use most
- how it helps you
- one problem it may cause

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use the **present simple** to talk about habits and facts.

---

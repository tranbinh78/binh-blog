# B1.2 – Present Perfect vs Past Simple

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Clearly distinguish **present perfect** and **past simple**
- Use both tenses naturally in conversation and writing
- Talk about experiences with or without a specific time reference

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### A Conversation About Experiences

**Anna:** Have you ever worked part-time before?  
**Ben:** Yes, I have worked in a small café.  
**Anna:** Really? When did you work there?  
**Ben:** I worked there last summer, during my university holiday.  
**Anna:** Did you enjoy it?  
**Ben:** Yes, I did. I learned how to communicate better with customers.

Anna has asked Ben about his **experience**, so she used the present perfect.  
When she wanted to know **when** it happened, she used the past simple.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. Why does Anna first use the present perfect?  
2. Why does she change to the past simple?  
3. What did Ben learn from his part-time job?

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Grammar Explanation

#### 🔹 Present Perfect
We use the **present perfect** to talk about:
- life experiences
- actions with no specific time
- situations that started in the past and continue now

**Form:**  
`have / has + past participle`

Examples:
- I have worked in different companies.
- She has learned many skills.
- We have never tried paragliding.

Common words:
- ever, never, already, yet, since, for

---

#### 🔹 Past Simple
We use the **past simple** to talk about:
- actions that happened at a **finished time**
- events in the past that are complete

**Form:**  
`verb + -ed` / irregular form

Examples:
- I started my first job five years ago.
- He worked in a café last summer.
- We finished the project yesterday.

Common time expressions:
- yesterday, last year, last week
- in 2022, two days ago

---

### 🔸 Compare the two

| Present Perfect | Past Simple |
|---------------|------------|
| I have worked in an office. | I worked in an office last year. |
| She has learned a lot. | She learned a lot during her first job. |

## ✍️ Part 5 – Writing (80–120 words)

### Topic: My Work or Learning Experience

Write about:
- an experience you have had
- when it happened (if necessary)
- what you learned from it

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use **present perfect** for experiences and  
**past simple** for specific events.

---

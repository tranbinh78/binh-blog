# B1.27 – Writing: Future Plans Essay

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a clear essay about future plans (120–150 words)
- Use future forms (**going to**, **will**) correctly
- Organize ideas logically with linking words
- Explain reasons and conditions for your plans

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Discuss the questions below:

1. What are your plans for the next five years?
2. Which plan is the most important for you?
3. What difficulties might you face in the future?

👉 Try to give **one plan + one reason**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Essay)

### My Future Plans

In the future, I am going to focus on my career and personal development.  
After finishing my studies, I plan to find a job related to my major so that I can gain practical experience.

I am also going to improve my English and communication skills because they are important in today’s working environment. If I have more free time, I will take online courses to learn new skills.

In conclusion, I believe careful planning will help me achieve my goals.  
If I work hard and stay motivated, I will have a successful and stable future.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. What is the writer’s main future plan?
2. Why does the writer want to improve English?
3. What condition does the writer mention for success?

---

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Useful Future Structures

**Going to** – plans and intentions  
- I am going to start a new job.
- She is going to study abroad.

**Will** – hopes, decisions, predictions  
- I will try my best.
- I will face challenges in the future.

**First Conditional**
- If + present simple, will + base verb  
- If I work hard, I will achieve my goals.

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Linking Words

- Ordering ideas: **first, then, after that**
- Adding ideas: **also, in addition**
- Giving reasons: **because, so that**
- Concluding: **in conclusion, overall**

Examples:
- I plan to study hard **so that** I can get a good job.
- **In conclusion**, planning is important for success.

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (120–150 words)

### Topic: My Future Plans

Write an essay about your future plans.

You should include:
- your main plans or goals
- reasons for these plans
- what you will do to achieve them
- possible challenges or conditions

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use **going to** for plans and **will** for hopes or predictions.  
Use **if** sentences to show conditions.

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1 – Introduction:**  
- Introduce your main future plans

**Paragraph 2 – Details:**  
- Explain your goals and reasons  
- Say what you will do to achieve them

**Paragraph 3 – Conclusion:**  
- Summarize your plans  
- Mention conditions for success

## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Learn basic vocabulary about daily activities
- Use the Present Simple to talk about routines

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Vocabulary: Daily Activities

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| wake up | thức dậy |
| get up | ra khỏi giường |
| get dressed | mặc quần áo |
| have breakfast | ăn sáng |
| go to work / school | đi làm / đi học |
| have lunch | ăn trưa |
| finish work | tan làm |
| relax | thư giãn |

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Present Simple

### Form
**Subject + verb**

- I wake up at six.
- She works in an office.

### Negative
- I do not work on Sunday.
- He does not wake up late.

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Adverbs of Frequency

always – usually – often – sometimes – never  

**Position:** before main verb  
- I usually wake up at six.
- She often relaxes in the evening.

With **to be**:
- He is always busy.

In this lesson, you will learn how to:

## 🧩 Part 1 – Vocabulary: Daily Activities

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|---------------|--------|
| wake up | thức dậy |
| get dressed | mặc quần áo |
| have breakfast | ăn sáng |
| go to work / school | đi làm / đi học |
| take a break | nghỉ giải lao |
| have lunch | ăn trưa |
| finish work | kết thúc công việc |
| relax | thư giãn |
| go to bed | đi ngủ |

📌 **Example sentences**
- I usually **wake up at 6 a.m.**
- She **finishes work at 5 p.m.**
- We often **relax in the evening**.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Adverbs of Frequency

### 🔹 Common adverbs
- always (luôn luôn)
- usually (thường xuyên)
- often (thường)
- sometimes (đôi khi)
- rarely (hiếm khi)
- never (không bao giờ)

### 🔹 Word order
👉 **Subject + adverb + verb**

**Examples**
- I **usually** drink coffee in the morning.
- He **often** goes to bed late.
- They **never** work on Sunday.

⚠️ With **to be**, adverbs come **after** the verb:
- She is **always** busy.

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Reading & Mini Dialogue

### 📖 Reading
> Minh works in an office. He usually starts work at 8 a.m.  
> He has lunch with his colleagues and finishes work at 5 p.m.  
> In the evening, he often watches TV or reads books before going to bed.

### 💬 Dialogue
**A:** What time do you usually wake up?  
**B:** I usually wake up at six.  
**A:** Do you work on weekends?  
**B:** No, I never work on Sundays.

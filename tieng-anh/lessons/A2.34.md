## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about a trip
- Practice travel and weather vocabulary

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Last summer, Minh went to the beach with his family.  
They stayed in a small hotel near the sea.  
The weather was sunny and hot.  
They swam and took many photos.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. Where did Minh go last summer?  
2. Who did he go with?  
3. What was the weather like?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice (Past Simple – Regular)

Choose the correct answer:

1. Minh ___ to the beach last summer.  
a) go  
b) went  

2. They ___ in a hotel.  
a) stay  
b) stayed  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete:
- Last summer, I went to ___.
- The weather was ___.

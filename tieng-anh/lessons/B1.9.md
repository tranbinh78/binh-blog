# B1.9 – Writing: Opinion Paragraph

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a clear opinion paragraph (100–120 words)
- Give reasons and simple examples to support your opinion
- Organize ideas logically using linking words

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Discuss the questions:
1. Do you think exams are the best way to evaluate students?
2. Should schools teach more practical skills?
3. Is online learning as effective as classroom learning?

👉 Try to give **one opinion + one reason** for each question.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Paragraph)

### Should Students Study Online or in Classrooms?

In my opinion, classroom learning is more effective than online learning for most students. When students study in classrooms, they can interact directly with teachers and classmates. This helps them stay focused and ask questions easily.

Moreover, classroom learning encourages teamwork and communication skills. Although online learning is convenient, it can make students feel isolated. For these reasons, I believe classroom learning is better for students’ overall development.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. What is the writer’s opinion?  
2. What are two reasons given to support this opinion?  
3. Why does the writer think online learning can be a problem?

---

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Structure of an Opinion Paragraph

A good opinion paragraph usually has **3 parts**:

1. **Opinion sentence**  
   - Clearly state your opinion  
   - Example: *In my opinion, exams are not the best way to evaluate students.*

2. **Supporting reasons**  
   - Give **2–3 reasons**  
   - Use simple examples if possible

3. **Conclusion sentence**  
   - Restate your opinion or give a final comment  
   - Example: *For these reasons, I believe schools should use other methods.*

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Linking Words

- Giving reasons: **because, since**
- Adding ideas: **also, moreover**
- Contrasting ideas: **however, although**
- Concluding: **for these reasons, in conclusion**

Examples:
- I think exams are stressful **because** they put too much pressure on students.
- **Moreover**, they do not show real abilities.

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (100–120 words)

### Topic: Your Opinion About Education

Choose **ONE** topic and write an opinion paragraph.

- Exams are the best way to evaluate students.
- Schools should teach more practical skills.
- Online learning is better than classroom learning.

You should:
- clearly state your opinion
- give **2–3 reasons**
- use linking words
- write **100–120 words**

🔔 **Tip:**  
Keep sentences clear and simple.  
Do not list ideas — explain them briefly.

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Sentence 1:**  
- State your opinion clearly

**Sentences 2–4:**  
- Give reasons and examples

**Sentence 5:**  
- Conclusion / final opinion

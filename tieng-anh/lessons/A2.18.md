## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text describing a person
- Practice adjectives in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Mai is my best friend.  
She is tall and young.  
She usually wears jeans and a white T-shirt.  
She likes simple and comfortable clothes.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. What does Mai look like?  
2. What clothes does she usually wear?  
3. What kind of clothes does she like?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. Mai is ___ and young.  
a) tall  
b) short  

2. She usually wears ___.  
a) a jeans  
b) jeans  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete:
- My friend is ___.
- He / She usually wears ___.

### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. What problem does the woman have?  
a) A headache  
b) A stomachache  

2. What does the doctor say?  
a) She should rest  
b) She should go to work  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. I have a ___.
2. You should ___ more water.

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking Practice

- Ask for advice.

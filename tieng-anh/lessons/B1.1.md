# B1.1 – Talking About Past Experiences

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about past experiences in a clear and natural way
- Use **present perfect** and **past simple** correctly
- Share a memorable experience in speaking and writing

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work in pairs or think individually.

1. Have you ever:
   - travelled to another city or country?
   - learned something difficult but interesting?
   - had a very happy or surprising day?

2. Answer these questions:
- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- Why do you remember it?

👉 Try to speak for **1–2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### A Memorable Experience

Last year, I had a very memorable experience during my summer holiday.  
I travelled to Da Nang with my family, and it was my first time visiting this city.

We stayed near the beach, so every morning we walked along the sea and enjoyed the fresh air. One day, we decided to try paragliding. At first, I was very nervous because I had never done it before. However, the instructor explained everything carefully, and I slowly felt more confident.

When I was in the air, I could see the beach, the city, and the mountains at the same time. It was an amazing feeling. After that day, I felt proud of myself because I had tried something new and challenging.

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. When did the writer travel to Da Nang?  
2. What activity made the writer nervous at first?  
3. Why did the writer feel proud after the experience?

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Grammar: Present Perfect vs Past Simple

**Present Perfect**
- We use it to talk about **experiences** (when the time is not important).
- Form: *have / has + past participle*

Examples:
- I have travelled to Da Nang.
- She has tried paragliding.

**Past Simple**
- We use it when the **time is mentioned or known**.
- Form: *verb + -ed* / irregular form

Examples:
- I travelled to Da Nang last year.
- I tried paragliding for the first time.

🔹 Compare:
- I have visited Da Nang.  
- I visited Da Nang last summer.

---

### 2️⃣ Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|----|----|
| experience | something that happens to you |
| nervous | worried or afraid |
| confident | sure about yourself |
| challenging | difficult but interesting |
| proud | feeling happy about an achievement |

## ✍️ Part 5 – Writing (80–120 words)

### Topic: A Memorable Experience

Write about an experience you remember clearly.
You can write about:
- what happened
- when it happened
- how you felt
- what you learned from it

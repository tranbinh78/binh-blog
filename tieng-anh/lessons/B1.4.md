# B1.4 – Jobs and Responsibilities

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about different jobs and responsibilities
- Describe what people do at work
- Use common verbs and expressions related to work and careers

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. What job do you do or want to do in the future?
2. What responsibilities does this job involve?
3. What skills are important for this job?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Different Jobs, Different Responsibilities

People choose different jobs for different reasons. Some people prefer office jobs because they are stable and predictable, while others enjoy practical jobs that allow them to work with their hands.

For example, an office worker usually deals with emails, meetings, and reports. They may need to communicate with clients and work in a team. A teacher, on the other hand, prepares lessons, explains ideas clearly, and supports students in their learning. Teachers often need patience and good communication skills.

In contrast, a technician or engineer may work with machines or technical systems. Their responsibilities include checking equipment, solving problems, and making sure everything works safely. Although these jobs can be challenging, they are also rewarding because workers can see the results of their efforts.

No matter what job people do, having clear responsibilities helps them work more effectively and feel more confident in their role.

1. Why do some people prefer office jobs?
2. What responsibilities does a teacher usually have?

- A. A teacher  
- B. An office worker  
- C. A technician  

2. What is one of his main responsibilities?
- A. Writing reports  
- B. Fixing technical problems  
- C. Teaching students

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Jobs & Responsibilities

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|-------------|--------|
| responsibility | a duty or task you must do |
| skill | the ability to do something well |
| communicate | to share information or ideas |
| deal with | to handle a task or problem |
| prepare | to get something ready |
| solve problems | to find answers to difficulties |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- I am responsible for checking reports.  
- My job involves working with customers.  
- I need to communicate clearly with my colleagues.  
- This job requires good teamwork skills.

👉 Try to make **2–3 sentences** about your own job or future job.

---

## ✍️ Part 5 – Short Writing (80–100 words)

### Topic: My Job or Dream Job

Write about:
- what your job is (or the job you want)
- your main responsibilities
- skills needed for this job
- why you like it

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use the **present simple** to talk about daily responsibilities.

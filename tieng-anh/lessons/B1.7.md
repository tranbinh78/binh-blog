## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### How Do People Learn Best?

Everyone learns in a different way. Some people prefer reading books or taking notes, while others learn better by listening to explanations or watching videos. There are also learners who understand information more easily when they practise and use it in real situations.

Good learning habits are important for all students. For example, studying regularly is more effective than studying only before exams. Taking short breaks can help learners stay focused, and setting clear goals makes learning more meaningful.

Another important habit is reviewing what you have learned. When students revise their lessons, they remember information for a longer time. Learning is not only about time, but also about using the right methods.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Learning & Education

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| learning habit | a regular way of studying |
| focus | to pay attention |
| revise / review | to study again |
| goal | something you want to achieve |
| method | a way of doing something |
| effective | producing a good result |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- I usually study in the morning / evening.
- I learn better when I practise regularly.
- Reviewing lessons helps me remember information.
- My goal is to improve my English skills.

👉 Make **3 sentences** about your own learning habits.

## ✍️ Part 5 – Short Writing (80–100 words)

### Topic: My Learning Habits

## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Use English in real-life situations
- Practice speaking and writing together

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Speaking Tasks

### Task 1 – Introduce Yourself
Talk about:
- your name
- your job or studies
- your free time

---

### Task 2 – Role-play

Situation:
- You are at a shop.
- Buy food and drinks.

Use:
- some / a / an
- polite questions

---

## ✍️ Part 2 – Writing Task (40–60 words)

**Topic:** My Daily Life  

Write about:
- your routine
- work or study
- free time

🔔 **Tip:**  
Write first, then check the sample.

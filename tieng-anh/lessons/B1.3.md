# B1.3 – Writing: A Memorable Experience

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a clear paragraph about a memorable experience (100–120 words)
- Use **present perfect** and **past simple** accurately
- Organize ideas logically (beginning – middle – ending)

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. What is the most memorable experience in your life?
2. When did it happen?
3. Why is it still important to you now?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.  
Focus on **what happened** and **what you learned**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Text)

### A Memorable Experience

Two years ago, I had a memorable experience when I took part in a volunteer project in my hometown.  
At that time, I worked with many people from different backgrounds, and we helped clean public areas and plant trees.

Since then, I have become more aware of environmental problems.  
This experience has taught me that small actions can make a big difference, and I still feel proud when I think about it.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. When did the experience happen?  
2. What activities did the writer do?  
3. What has the writer learned from this experience?

---

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Review

### 🔍 Tense Check

Find examples of:
- **Past simple** (specific time / finished actions)
- **Present perfect** (result or meaning now)

Examples from the text:
- Past simple: *took part, worked, helped*  
- Present perfect: *have become, has taught*

👉 Notice how the tenses work together to tell a complete story.

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (100–120 words)

### Topic: A Memorable Experience

Write about **one memorable experience** in your life.

You should include:
- what the experience was
- when and where it happened
- how you felt at the time
- what you have learned from it

🔔 **Writing Tips**
- Use **past simple** for events and actions
- Use **present perfect** for lessons or changes that are still true
- Organize your paragraph clearly

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1:**  
- Introduce the experience (when, where, what)

**Paragraph 2:**  
- Describe what happened and how you felt

**Paragraph 3:**  
- Explain what you have learned and why it is important now

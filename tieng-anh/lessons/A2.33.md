## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Learn basic travel and weather vocabulary
- Talk about future plans using going to

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar: Going to (Plans)

### Form
- I am going to travel next week.
- We are going to stay in a hotel.

### Questions
- What are you going to do?
- Where are you going to stay?

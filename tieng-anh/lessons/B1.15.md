# B1.15 – Writing: Health Advice Article

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a short health advice article (120–150 words)
- Give clear advice using **should / shouldn’t / if**
- Organize ideas logically with headings and linking words

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Discuss the questions below:

1. What health problems are common among students or office workers?
2. What advice would you give to someone who feels tired or stressed?
3. Where do people usually get health advice from?

👉 Try to give **one problem + one piece of advice**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Article)

### How to Stay Healthy in a Busy Life

Many people have busy lives and do not have enough time to take care of their health. However, small changes can help improve both physical and mental health.

First, people should try to exercise regularly, even if it is only for 20 minutes a day. Walking, cycling, or doing simple exercises can help reduce stress and increase energy. Second, it is important to eat balanced meals and avoid too much fast food.

In addition, getting enough sleep is necessary for good health. If people sleep well, they can concentrate better and feel more positive. In conclusion, living healthily does not require big changes, but good daily habits.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. Why do many people find it difficult to stay healthy?
2. What two pieces of advice are mentioned in the article?
3. Why is sleep important according to the text?

---

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Language for Giving Health Advice

- People **should** exercise regularly.
- People **shouldn’t** eat too much fast food.
- **If** you feel tired, you should take a short break.
- It’s important to / It’s a good idea to …

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Linking Words

- Ordering ideas: **first, second**
- Adding ideas: **in addition, also**
- Concluding: **in conclusion**

Examples:
- First, people should exercise regularly.
- In conclusion, good habits help improve health.

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (120–150 words)

### Topic: How to Live a Healthier Life

Write a **health advice article** for students or office workers.

You should:
- introduce the problem
- give **2–3 pieces of advice**
- explain why the advice is useful
- write a short conclusion

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use **general language** (people, students, workers) instead of “I”.

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1 – Introduction:**  
- Introduce the health problem or situation

**Paragraph 2 – Advice:**  
- Give 2–3 pieces of advice with explanations

**Paragraph 3 – Conclusion:**  
- Summarize the advice and encourage healthy habits

Nowadays, many students and office workers feel tired and stressed because of their busy schedules. This can affect their health if they do not change their habits.

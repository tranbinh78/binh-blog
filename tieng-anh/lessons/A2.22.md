## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text describing a place
- Practice There is / There are in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

My neighbourhood is very nice.  
There is a supermarket near my house.  
There are two small restaurants on the main street.  
The park is opposite the school.  
I often go there in the evening.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. What is near the writer’s house?  
2. How many restaurants are there?  
3. Where is the park?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. There ___ a supermarket near my house.  
a) is  
b) are  

2. There are two ___ on the street.  
a) restaurant  
b) restaurants  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete:
- There is a ___ near my house.
- There are ___ on my street.

---

## ✅ What you can do now
✔ Understand place descriptions  
✔ Use There is / There are correctly

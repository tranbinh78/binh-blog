- Learn vocabulary for common health problems
- Use should / shouldn’t to give advice

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Giving Advice (should / shouldn’t)

### Form
- You **should** see a doctor.
- You **should** drink more water.
- You **shouldn’t** work too hard.
- You **shouldn’t** stay up late.

### Asking for advice
- What should I do?
- Should I see a doctor?

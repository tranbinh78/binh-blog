## 🧠 Overview – What You Will Do

In this final task, you will:
- Read and understand a real-life situation

- Speak about your ideas and solutions
- Write a short, practical text

👉 This task reflects **real communication**, not isolated grammar practice.

---

## 📖 Part 1 – Reading: Real-life Situation

### Situation: Busy Life and Stress

Many people today feel stressed because of work, study, and family responsibilities. They often do not have enough time to relax or take care of their health. As a result, communication problems and health issues may appear.

People need to find ways to manage their time better, communicate clearly, and maintain a balanced lifestyle in order to live and work more effectively.

---

### 📘 Reading Questions

1. Why do many people feel stressed today?
2. What problems can stress cause?
3. What do people need to do to improve their lives?

---

## 🎧 Part 2 – Listening: Daily Life Conversation

### 🎧 Listening Task
You will hear a conversation between two colleagues talking about their daily routines.

**Task:** Choose the correct answer (A, B, or C).

1. What problem does the speaker mention?
- A. Lack of money  
- B. Lack of time  
- C. Lack of motivation  

2. What solution is suggested?
- A. Working longer hours  
- B. Planning time better  
- C. Changing jobs  

3. What will the speaker do next?
- A. Ignore the problem  
- B. Follow the advice  
- C. Ask someone else

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking: Real-life Communication

### Task 1 – Personal Situation (2–3 minutes)

Talk about:
- one problem in your daily life (stress, time, communication, health)
- why it happens
- what you are going to do to solve it

👉 Use:
- **should / shouldn’t**
- **going to / will**
- at least **one if sentence**

---

### Task 2 – Opinion & Advice (1–2 minutes)

Situation:  
Your friend feels stressed because of work or study.

Give advice:
- what they should do
- why it is helpful
- what may happen if they follow your advice

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing: Practical Communication (120–150 words)

### Task: Writing an Advice Email

Write an email to a friend or colleague who feels stressed because of a busy life.

You should:
- explain the problem briefly
- give **2–3 pieces of advice**
- use polite and supportive language
- write a clear closing

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1:**  
- Greeting + problem

**Paragraph 2:**  
- Advice + reasons

**Paragraph 3:**  
- Encouraging closing

---

### 📄 Sample Writing (Optional)

<details>
<summary><strong>Show / Hide Sample Email</strong></summary>

**Subject:** Some Advice to Reduce Stress

Dear Minh,

I’m sorry to hear that you are feeling stressed because of your busy schedule.  
Many people face the same problem when they do not have enough time to rest.

I think you should try planning your time more carefully and taking short breaks during the day. It’s also a good idea to exercise regularly and talk to someone when you feel tired. If you follow these habits, you will feel more relaxed and confident.

I hope this advice helps.  
Best regards,  
Nam

</details>

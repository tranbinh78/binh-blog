# B1.5 – Job Interviews

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Understand common interview questions
- Talk about your skills, experience, and strengths
- Answer job interview questions more confidently and politely

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. Have you ever had a job interview?
2. What questions do you think interviewers usually ask?
3. What is the most difficult part of a job interview for you?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### A Job Interview

**Interviewer:** Good morning. Please tell me about yourself.  
**Candidate:** Good morning. I recently graduated from university, and I am very interested in this position. I have some experience working part-time, and I enjoy working in a team.

**Interviewer:** What are your strengths?  
**Candidate:** I am responsible and hardworking. I can communicate well with others, and I am always willing to learn new skills.

**Interviewer:** Why do you want this job?  
**Candidate:** I believe this job is a good opportunity for me to use my skills and gain more experience in a professional environment.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. What experience does the candidate mention?  
2. Which strengths does the candidate talk about?  
3. Why does the candidate want the job?

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Common Interview Questions

- Can you tell me about yourself?
- What experience do you have?
- What are your strengths?
- What are your weaknesses?
- Why do you want this job?

👉 Practice answering these questions in simple and clear sentences.

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Answers & Expressions

- I have experience working as a …  
- I am responsible for …  
- My main strength is …  
- I am willing to learn new skills.  
- I believe this job is suitable for me.

---

### 3️⃣ Grammar Note: Present Simple & Present Perfect

- **Present simple**: talk about current skills and responsibilities  
  - I work well in a team.
- **Present perfect**: talk about experience  
  - I have worked as an office assistant.

---

## ✍️ Part 5 – Speaking Practice (Interview Role-play)

### Activity

Work in pairs.

**Student A (Interviewer):**
- Ask at least **4 interview questions**

**Student B (Candidate):**
- Answer clearly and politely
- Talk about your experience and strengths

Then change roles.

### Topic: My Strengths for a Job

Write about:
- the job you want
- your experience
- your strengths
- why you are suitable for this job

# B1.22 – Environmental Issues

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about common environmental problems
- Understand why environmental protection is important
- Use basic vocabulary related to the environment

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. What environmental problems can you see in your city or country?
2. Do you do anything to protect the environment?
3. Why should people care about environmental issues?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Environmental Problems Today

Environmental problems are becoming more serious around the world. Air pollution, water pollution, and plastic waste affect both people and nature. Many cities have dirty air because of traffic and factories, which can cause health problems.

Another major issue is plastic waste. People use a lot of plastic bags and bottles every day, but many of them are not recycled. As a result, plastic pollutes rivers, oceans, and land. This pollution harms animals and damages natural environments.

Protecting the environment is important for our future. If people change small habits, such as reducing plastic use and saving energy, they can help protect the planet and improve their quality of life.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Environmental Issues

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| environment | the natural world around us |
| pollution | harmful substances in air, water, or land |
| plastic waste | used plastic materials |
| recycle | to reuse materials |
| protect | to keep something safe |
| energy | power used for work or activities |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- Pollution is a serious environmental problem.
- Plastic waste harms animals and nature.
- People should recycle more to protect the environment.
- Small actions can make a big difference.

👉 Make **3 sentences** about environmental problems or protection.

## ✍️ Part 5 – Short Writing (80–100 words)

### Topic: An Environmental Problem

Write about:
- one environmental problem
- why it is serious
- what people can do to help

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use the **present simple** to talk about facts and general ideas.

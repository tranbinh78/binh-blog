## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short conversation comparing people
- Use basic comparatives (taller, shorter)

### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. Who is taller?  
a) Anna  
b) Lisa  

2. Who is wearing a jacket?  
a) Anna  
b) Lisa  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. Anna is ___ than Lisa.  
2. Lisa is wearing a ___.

## 💬 Part 2 – Dialogue Practice

Practice the conversation with a partner.

---

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking Practice

Answer:
1. Who is taller in your family?
2. What clothes do you usually wear?

---

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task

Compare two people you know (4–5 sentences).  
Use:
- tall / short
- taller than / shorter than

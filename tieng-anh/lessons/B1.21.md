# B1.21 – Writing: Opinion Email

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a clear and polite opinion email (100–120 words)
- Express agreement or disagreement respectfully
- Support your opinion with reasons and examples
- Use appropriate email language and structure

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Discuss the questions below:

1. When do people write emails to express opinions?
2. Is it different to share opinions in emails and in conversations? Why?
3. What makes an email sound polite?

👉 Try to give **one idea + one reason** for each question.

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Email)

### Subject: Opinion about Online Meetings

Dear Ms. Lan,

I am writing to share my opinion about online meetings at work.  
In my opinion, online meetings are very convenient because they save time and reduce travel.

However, I feel that too many online meetings can be tiring. Sometimes it is difficult to stay focused for a long time. I believe short and well-planned meetings are more effective.

Thank you for considering my opinion.  
I look forward to your response.

Kind regards,  
Minh

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. What is the topic of the email?
2. What advantage of online meetings is mentioned?
3. What suggestion does the writer give?

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Structure of an Opinion Email

A clear opinion email usually includes:

1. **Subject line** – shows the topic clearly  
2. **Greeting** – polite opening (Dear …)  
3. **Introduction** – reason for writing  
4. **Opinion + reasons** – agreement/disagreement with explanation  
5. **Closing** – polite ending

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Email Expressions

**Opening**
- I am writing to share my opinion about …
- I would like to express my view on …

**Giving opinions**
- In my opinion, …
- I believe that …
- From my point of view, …

**Agreeing / Disagreeing politely**
- I agree with … because …
- I see your point, but …
- Although I understand …, I think …

**Closing**
- Thank you for your time and consideration.
- I look forward to hearing from you.
- Kind regards / Best regards,

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (100–120 words)

### Topic: Writing an Opinion Email

Write an **opinion email** about ONE topic:

- Online learning at school
- Working in groups
- Using smartphones in class

You should:
- clearly state your opinion
- give **2 reasons**
- use polite language
- write a clear closing

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1:**  
- Greeting + reason for writing

**Paragraph 2:**  
- Your opinion + reasons

**Paragraph 3:**  
- Polite closing

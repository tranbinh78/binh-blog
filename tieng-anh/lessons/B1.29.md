## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### A Balanced Life in a Busy World

Many people today have busy lives because of work, study, and technology. As a result, they often feel stressed and do not have enough time for themselves. However, having a balanced lifestyle is important for both physical and mental health.

To achieve balance, people should manage their time carefully. For example, they can set clear goals, take short breaks, and avoid spending too much time on screens. In addition, regular exercise and good communication with family and friends can help reduce stress.

If people learn how to balance work and personal life, they will feel happier and more productive in the long term.

## ✍️ Part 5 – Writing (100–120 words)

### Topic: Improving My Daily Life

Write a short paragraph about:
- one problem in your daily life
- what you have done or will do to improve it
- why this change is helpful

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use:
- **present perfect** for experience
- **future forms** for plans
- **if** for conditions

---

### 📄 Sample Writing (Optional)

<details>
<summary><strong>Show / Hide Sample Writing</strong></summary>

In my daily life, I sometimes feel stressed because of work and lack of exercise.  
Recently, I have tried to manage my time better by planning my tasks in advance. I have also started walking every evening.

If I continue these habits, I will feel healthier and more relaxed.  
I believe small changes like these can improve my quality of life and help me stay motivated.

# B1.8 – Giving Opinions About Education

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Give and explain your opinions about education
- Agree and disagree politely
- Use common opinion expressions clearly and naturally

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. Do you think education is more important than experience?
2. Should students study online or in the classroom?
3. What is one thing you would like to change in schools?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.  
Focus on **giving reasons** for your opinions.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Different Opinions About Education

People often have different opinions about education. Some believe that formal education is the best way to gain knowledge, while others think that real-life experience is more valuable.

For example, many students enjoy online learning because it is flexible and convenient. They can study at their own pace and save time travelling. However, other students prefer studying in classrooms. They feel more motivated when they can interact with teachers and classmates.

In my opinion, both forms of education have advantages. The best choice depends on students’ needs, learning styles, and personal situations.

---

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Giving Opinions

Common expressions:
- In my opinion, …
- I think / I believe that …
- From my point of view, …
- As far as I am concerned, …

Examples:
- In my opinion, education should focus on practical skills.
- I believe that teachers play an important role in learning.

---

### 2️⃣ Agreeing and Disagreeing Politely

**Agreeing**
- I agree with you.
- That’s a good point.
- I think so too.

**Disagreeing politely**
- I’m not sure about that.
- I see your point, but …
- I agree to some extent, but …

Examples:
- I see your point, but classroom learning helps me focus better.
- I agree to some extent, but experience is also very important.

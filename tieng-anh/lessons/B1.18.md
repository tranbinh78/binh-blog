# B1.18 – Writing: Travel Blog Post

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Write a short travel blog post (120–150 words)
- Describe places, activities, and feelings clearly
- Organize ideas with a friendly blog style and linking words

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Discuss the questions below:

1. Do you like reading travel blogs? Why or why not?
2. What information do you expect from a travel blog?
3. Which place would you like to write about?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading (Model Blog Post)

### A Weekend in Hoi An

Last weekend, I had a wonderful trip to Hoi An, one of the most beautiful towns in Vietnam. It was my first time visiting this place, and I was really impressed by its peaceful atmosphere.

During the trip, I walked around the old town, visited traditional houses, and tried many local dishes. In the evening, the streets were full of colourful lanterns, which made the town look magical.

I really enjoyed this trip because it helped me relax and learn more about local culture. If you are looking for a quiet and beautiful destination, Hoi An is a great choice.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. When did the writer visit Hoi An?
2. What activities did the writer do?
3. Why does the writer recommend Hoi An?

---

## 🧠 Part 3 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Useful Language for Travel Blogs

- It was my first time visiting …
- I was impressed by …
- One of the highlights of the trip was …
- I really enjoyed …
- I would recommend this place to …

---

### 2️⃣ Linking Words for Blogs

- Ordering events: **first, then, after that**
- Adding ideas: **also, in addition**
- Giving opinions: **I think, I believe**
- Concluding: **overall, in the end**

Examples:
- First, we visited the old town. Then, we tried local food.
- Overall, it was a memorable trip.

---

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (120–150 words)

### Topic: A Travel Experience You Would Like to Share

Write a **travel blog post** about a place you visited.

You should include:
- where and when you went
- who you went with
- what you did and saw
- how you felt
- why you recommend (or don’t recommend) the place

🔔 **Tip:**  
Write in a **friendly and personal tone**, as if you are sharing your experience online.

---

### 🧩 Writing Plan (Suggested)

**Paragraph 1 – Introduction:**  
- Introduce the place and time of the trip

**Paragraph 2 – Main experience:**  
- Activities, sights, food, people

**Paragraph 3 – Feelings & recommendation:**  
- Your opinion and suggestion for readers

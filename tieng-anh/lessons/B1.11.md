# B1.11 – Advantages and Disadvantages

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about advantages and disadvantages of a topic
- Use common structures to compare positive and negative points
- Give balanced opinions with reasons and examples

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### The Advantages and Disadvantages of Technology

Technology has changed the way people live, work, and communicate. One major advantage of technology is convenience. People can access information quickly, communicate easily, and complete tasks more efficiently. For example, online tools allow employees to work from home and students to learn at their own pace.

Another advantage is improved communication. Social media and messaging apps help people stay connected with friends and family, even when they live far away.

However, technology also has disadvantages. One important problem is overuse. Spending too much time on screens can affect health, causing eye strain, stress, and lack of physical activity. In addition, people may become too dependent on technology and lose face-to-face communication skills.

In conclusion, while technology offers many benefits, it is important to use it carefully and responsibly.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Structures for Advantages & Disadvantages

**Talking about advantages**
- One advantage of … is that …
- … helps people to …
- … makes it easier to …

**Talking about disadvantages**
- One disadvantage of … is that …
- … can cause problems such as …
- … may lead to …

Examples:
- One advantage of online learning is that students can study anytime.
- One disadvantage of technology is that people may become less active.

---

### 2️⃣ Linking Words

- Adding ideas: **also, moreover**
- Contrasting ideas: **however, on the other hand**
- Concluding: **in conclusion**

Examples:
- Technology is convenient; however, it can also be distracting.
- On the other hand, some people prefer traditional methods.

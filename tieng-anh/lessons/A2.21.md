---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: There is / There are

### Form
- There is a bank near my house.
- There are two restaurants on this street.

### Questions
- Is there a park near here?
- Are there any shops around here?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Prepositions of Place

- next to
- near
- opposite
- behind
- in front of

Examples:
- The bank is next to the post office.
- The park is opposite the school.

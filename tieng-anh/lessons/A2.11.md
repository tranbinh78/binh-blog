### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. What is Anna’s hobby?  
a) Reading books  
b) Playing sports  
c) Watching TV  

2. What doesn’t Tom like?  
a) Reading  
b) Sports  
c) Music  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. Anna likes ___ books.  
2. Tom doesn’t like ___.

---

## 📄 Listening Script (Optional)

<details>
<summary><strong>Show / Hide Listening Script</strong></summary>

Anna: What do you do in your free time, Tom?  
Tom: I like listening to music, but I don’t like sports.  
Anna: How about reading?  
Tom: Not really. What about you?  
Anna: I love reading books in the evening.

</details>

## 💬 Part 2 – Dialogue Practice

Practice the conversation with a partner.

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking Practice

Answer:
1. What do you do in your free time?
2. What do you like or don’t like?

---

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task

Talk about your hobbies (5–6 sentences).  
Use:
- free time vocabulary
- like / don’t like

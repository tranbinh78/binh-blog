## 🎯 Lesson Objective
- Understand a short text about work or study
- Practice Present Simple in context

---

## 🧩 Part 1 – Reading

Minh works in a small company.  
He starts work at eight o’clock and finishes at five.  
He usually has lunch with his colleagues.  
In the evening, he sometimes studies English at home.

---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Comprehension

1. Where does Minh work?  
2. What time does he finish work?  
3. What does he do in the evening?

---

## 🧩 Part 3 – Grammar Practice

Choose the correct answer:

1. Minh ___ in a company.  
a) work  
b) works  

2. He sometimes ___ English.  
a) study  
b) studies  

---

## 🧩 Part 4 – Controlled Writing

Complete about you:

- I work / study at ___.
- I start at ___.
- In the evening, I usually ___.

✔ Understand a work/study text

# B1.17 – Cultural Differences

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Understand common cultural differences between countries
- Talk about customs, traditions, and daily behaviour
- Compare cultures using simple language

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. Have you ever met people from another country?
2. What cultural differences did you notice?
3. Do you think cultural differences are interesting or difficult? Why?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### Understanding Cultural Differences

When people travel or work with others from different countries, they may notice cultural differences. These differences can be seen in the way people communicate, eat, dress, or behave in daily life.

For example, in some cultures, people greet each other with a handshake, while in others they bow or simply say hello. In many Western countries, being on time is very important, but in some places people are more relaxed about time.

Food culture is another area with many differences. In some countries, people usually eat together and share dishes, while in others each person has their own plate. Understanding these differences helps people avoid misunderstandings and communicate more politely.

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Culture & Customs

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| culture | the customs and traditions of a society |
| custom | a traditional way of behaving |
| greeting | saying hello to someone |
| behaviour | the way people act |
| polite | showing respect to others |
| misunderstanding | a wrong idea about something |

---

### 2️⃣ Comparing Cultures

Useful structures:
- In my country, people usually …  
- In some cultures, it is common to …  
- Compared to my country, people in …  

Examples:
- In my country, people often eat together as a family.
- Compared to my country, people in Japan are more punctual.

👉 Make **3 sentences** comparing your culture with another culture.

## 🗣️ Part 5 – Speaking Practice

### Activity: Cultural Comparison

Work in pairs.

**Student A:**  
Talk about customs in your country (greetings, food, time).

**Student B:**  
Talk about customs in another country you know.

Then discuss:
- What is similar?
- What is different?

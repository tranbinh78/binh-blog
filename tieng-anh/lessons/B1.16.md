# B1.16 – Travel Experiences

## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
- Talk about travel experiences in the past
- Describe places, activities, and feelings when travelling
- Use common travel-related vocabulary naturally

---

## 🗣️ Part 1 – Warm-up (Speaking)

Work with a partner or think individually.

Answer the questions:
1. Have you ever travelled to another city or country?
2. Where did you go and who did you travel with?
3. What was the most interesting part of the trip?

👉 Try to speak for **2 minutes**.

---

## 📖 Part 2 – Reading

### A Memorable Trip

Last summer, I travelled to Hoi An with my friends for a short holiday. It was my first time visiting this ancient town, and I was really impressed by its beauty and atmosphere.

During the trip, we walked around the old town, visited traditional houses, and tried local food. In the evening, the streets were full of colourful lanterns, and the town became very lively. We also talked to local people, who were friendly and helpful.

This trip was memorable because it helped me relax and learn more about Vietnamese culture. Since then, I have become more interested in travelling and discovering new places.

---

### 📘 Comprehension Questions

1. When did the writer travel to Hoi An?
2. What activities did the writer do during the trip?
3. Why was the trip memorable for the writer?

## 🧠 Part 4 – Language Focus

### 1️⃣ Vocabulary: Travel & Tourism

| Word / Phrase | Meaning |
|--------------|--------|
| travel experience | something that happens during a trip |
| ancient town | a very old town with history |
| local food | food from a specific area |
| culture | customs and traditions of a place |
| impress | to make someone feel admiration |
| discover | to find something new |

---

### 2️⃣ Useful Sentences

- I travelled to … last year.
- It was my first time visiting …
- I was impressed by the culture and people.
- The trip helped me relax and learn new things.

👉 Make **3 sentences** about your own travel experience.

---

## ✍️ Part 5 – Short Writing (80–100 words)

### Topic: My Travel Experience

Write about:
- where you went
- who you travelled with
- what you did
- how you felt about the trip

🔔 **Tip:**  
Use the **past simple** to describe events.

---

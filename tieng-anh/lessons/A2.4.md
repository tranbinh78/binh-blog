- Review vocabulary and grammar

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar Review

Choose the correct answer:

1. I usually ___ up at six.  
a) wake  
b) wakes  

2. She ___ watch TV in the evening.  
a) often  
b) never

## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (40–60 words)

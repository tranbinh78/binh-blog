## ✍️ Part 4 – Writing Task (40–60 words)

**Topic:** My Daily Routine  

Write about:
- wake-up time
- work or study
- evening activity

🔔 **Tip:**  
Write your own paragraph first.  
Then you can open the sample to check.

---

## 📄 Sample Writing (Optional)

<details>
<summary><strong>Show / Hide Sample Writing</strong></summary>

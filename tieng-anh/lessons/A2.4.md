---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar Check

Choose the correct answer:

1. I ___ wake up early.  
a) usually  
b) never  

2. She ___ work on Sunday.  
a) doesn’t  
b) don’t  

---

## ✍️ Part 3 – Writing Task

Write a short paragraph (40–60 words):

**Topic:** My Daily Routine  
Include:
- wake-up time
- work or study
- evening activity

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task (Can-do)

1. Your daily routine  
2. Ask your partner about their routine

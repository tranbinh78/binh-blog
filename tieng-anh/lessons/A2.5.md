---

## 🧩 Part 2 – Grammar: Possessive Adjectives

| Subject | Possessive |
|------|-----------|
| I | my |
| you | your |
| he | his |
| she | her |
| we | our |
| they | their |

### Examples
- This is **my** father.
- She is **her** sister.
- They live with **their** parents.

## 🧩 Part 3 – Describing People (Basic)

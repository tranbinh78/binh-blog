- Give simple directions using imperatives

### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. Where does the man want to go?  
a) The bank  
b) The hospital  
c) The post office  

2. Where is the place?  
a) Next to the park  
b) Opposite the school  
c) Near the supermarket  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. Go ___ ahead.  
2. The bank is ___ the school.

---

## 💬 Part 2 – Dialogue Practice

## 🗣️ Part 3 – Speaking Practice

Answer:
1. Is there a supermarket near your house?
2. Where is it?

---

## 🗣️ Part 4 – Speaking Task

Role-play:
- Ask for directions to a place.
- Give directions using:
  - go straight
  - turn left / right
  - next to / opposite

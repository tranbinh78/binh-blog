### 🎧 Listening Task 1 – Listen and choose

1. Where is Anna going to travel?  
a) To the beach  
b) To the city  

2. What will the weather be like?  
a) Rainy  
b) Sunny  

---

### 🎧 Listening Task 2 – Listen and complete

1. Anna is going to ___ next week.  
2. The weather will be ___.

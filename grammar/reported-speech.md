
# Reported Speech (Câu tường thuật)

## 1. Definition  
**English:**  
Reported speech is used to report what someone said without quoting their exact words.<br>
It usually involves changes in pronouns, tense, time expressions, and sometimes word order.<br>

**Tiếng Việt:**  
Câu tường thuật dùng để kể lại lời nói của ai đó mà *không lặp lại nguyên văn*.<br>
Thường phải thay đổi đại từ, thì, trạng từ chỉ thời gian và một số cấu trúc.<br>


## 2. Direct vs. Reported Speech

### ✔ Direct Speech (Lời nói trực tiếp)
EN: She said, “I am tired.”<br>
VI: Cô ấy nói, “Tôi mệt.”<br>

### ✔ Reported Speech (Lời nói gián tiếp)
EN: She said that she was tired.<br>
VI: Cô ấy nói rằng cô ấy mệt.<br>


## 3. Changes in Tense (Quy tắc đổi thì)

| Direct | Reported |<br>
|--------|----------|<br>
| am / is → was | “I am happy” → He said he was happy. |<br>
| are → were | |<br>
| do → did | |<br>
| will → would | |<br>
| can → could | |<br>
| have → had | |<br>
| past simple → past perfect | “I bought it” → She said she had bought it. |<br>


## 4. Changes in Pronouns (Đổi đại từ)
- I → he / she  
- my → his / her  
- we → they  
- our → their  

Example:  
EN: “I love my job,” she said.<br>
→ She said that she loved her job.<br>


## 5. Time & Place Changes  
| Direct | Reported |<br>
|--------|----------|<br>
| today → that day |<br>
| yesterday → the day before |<br>
| tomorrow → the next day |<br>
| now → then |<br>
| here → there |<br>

Example:  
EN: “I will come tomorrow,” he said.<br>
→ He said that he would come the next day.<br>


## 6. Reporting Verbs (Động từ tường thuật)
- say  
- tell  
- ask  
- advise  
- warn  
- remind  

Example:  
EN: “Close the door,” he said.<br>
→ He told me to close the door.<br>


## 7. Reporting Questions (Tường thuật câu hỏi)

### ✔ Yes/No Questions  
Use **ask + if / whether**<br>
EN: “Do you like tea?”<br>
→ She asked if I liked tea.<br>

### ✔ WH- Questions  
Use **ask + WH-word + clause**<br>
EN: “Where do you live?”<br>
→ She asked where I lived.<br>


## 8. Reporting Commands (Câu mệnh lệnh)
Use **tell/ask + object + to V**<br>

Example:  
EN: “Please sit down.”<br>
→ He asked me to sit down.<br>

EN: “Don’t go!”<br>
→ She told me not to go.<br>

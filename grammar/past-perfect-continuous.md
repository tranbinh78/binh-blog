# Past Perfect Continuous

## 1. Định nghĩa
**English:** The Past Perfect Continuous describes an action that had been in progress before another past action; it emphasizes duration.  
**Tiếng Việt:** Thì quá khứ hoàn thành tiếp diễn mô tả hành động đã tiếp diễn trước một hành động khác trong quá khứ, nhấn mạnh khoảng thời gian.

---

## 2. Công thức kèm ví dụ

### 🔸 Khẳng định
**Công thức:**  
- S + had been + V-ing  
**Ví dụ:**  
- EN: She had been studying for two hours before the test.  
- VI: Cô ấy đã học trong hai giờ trước khi kiểm tra.  

---

### 🔸 Phủ định
**Công thức:**  
- S + had not been + V-ing  
**Ví dụ:**  
- EN: They hadn’t been living there long.  
- VI: Họ sống ở đó chưa lâu.  

---

### 🔸 Nghi vấn
**Công thức:**  
- Had + S + been + V-ing?  
**Ví dụ:**  
- EN: Had you been waiting long?  
- VI: Bạn đã chờ lâu chưa?  

---

## 3. Cách dùng
- Hành động kéo dài trước một thời điểm quá khứ khác.  
- Nhấn mạnh thời lượng (for / since).  
- Thường dùng để giải thích nguyên nhân (He was tired because…).

---

## 4. Dấu hiệu nhận biết
for, since, how long, before

---

## 5. Bài tập (10 câu)

### A. Điền (gợi ý)
1. She ___ for two hours before the test. (**study**)  
2. They ___ there long. (**not/live**)  
3. I ___ when he arrived. (**already/work**)  
4. ___ you ___ long? (**wait**)  
5. He ___ about the problem. (**think**)  

### B. Multiple-choice
6. She ____ for hours before the meeting.  
a. had been working  b. had worked  c. worked  d. has worked  
7. I ____ there since morning.  
a. had been waiting  b. have waited  c. waited  d. am waiting  
8. ____ they ____ before he came?  
a. Had / been living  b. Have / been living  c. Had / lived  d. Did / live  
9. He ____ tired because he ____ all day.  
a. was / had been working  b. is / has worked  c. was / worked  d. were / working  
10. She ____ before he arrived.  
a. had been sleeping  b. slept  c. has been sleeping  d. sleeps

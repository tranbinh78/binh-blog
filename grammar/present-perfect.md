# Present Perfect

## 1. Định nghĩa
Used for experiences, unfinished time, or results.  
Dùng cho trải nghiệm, hành động chưa kết thúc, hoặc kết quả.

## 2. Công thức + ví dụ

### 🔸 Khẳng định
S + have/has + P2  
**Example:** EN: I have visited Japan.  
VI: Tôi đã từng đến Nhật.

### 🔸 Phủ định
S + have/has not + P2  
**Example:** EN: She hasn’t finished her homework.  
VI: Cô ấy chưa hoàn thành bài tập.

### 🔸 Nghi vấn
Have/Has + S + P2?  
**Example:** EN: Have you seen this movie?  
VI: Bạn đã xem phim này chưa?

## 3. Cách dùng
1. Experiences → I’ve tried sushi.  
2. Unfinished time → She has worked today.  
3. Results → He has broken his leg.

## 4. Signal
ever, never, already, yet, just, recently

## 5. Bài tập
1. I ___ this film. (**not/see**)  
2. She ___ dinner. (**make**)  
3. ___ you ___ Japan? (**ever/visit**)  
4. They ___ the homework. (**finish**)  
5. He ___ his key. (**lose**)  

6. I ____ this movie.  
a. have see b. have seen c. has seen d. seen  
7. She ____ her work.  
a. have finished b. finished c. has finished d. finishes  
8. ____ you ____ him?  
a. Do/meet b. Have/met c. Have/meet d. Has/meet  
9. He ____ his leg.  
a. has broken b. broke c. break d. breaking  
10. They ____ the homework.  
a. haven’t finished b. hasn’t finished c. not finish d. didn’t finish


# WH-Questions (Câu hỏi WH)

## 1. Definition  
**English:**  
WH-questions are questions that begin with WH‑words such as: *what, where, when, who, whom, which, why, how*.  
They are used to ask for specific information rather than Yes/No answers.

**Tiếng Việt:**  
Câu hỏi WH bắt đầu bằng các từ để hỏi như *what, where, when, who, which, why, how*…  
Dùng để hỏi thông tin chi tiết, không trả lời bằng Yes/No.

---

## 2. WH-Question Words (Các từ để hỏi)

| WH-word | Meaning | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| what | cái gì | What are you doing? |
| where | ở đâu | Where do you live? |
| when | khi nào | When will she arrive? |
| who | ai | Who is calling? |
| whom | ai (tân ngữ) | Whom did you meet? |
| which | cái nào | Which book do you like? |
| why | tại sao | Why are you sad? |
| how | như thế nào | How do you make this? |

---

## 3. Structure  
### ✔ 1. WH-word + auxiliary + subject + verb  
**Example:**  
EN: Where do you live?  
VI: Bạn sống ở đâu?

### ✔ 2. WH-word + be + subject  
**Example:**  
EN: Who is that?  
VI: Đó là ai?

### ✔ 3. WH-word + modal + subject + verb  
**Example:**  
EN: What should I do?  
VI: Tôi nên làm gì?

---

## 4. Special Cases  
### ✔ WH + to V (hỏi gián tiếp)  
EN: I don’t know what to do.  
VI: Tôi không biết phải làm gì.

### ✔ WH-question without auxiliary when WH is subject  
EN: Who broke the window?  
(not: ❌ Who did break the window?)

### ✔ WH + ever (nhấn mạnh)  
whatever, wherever, whenever…  
EN: You can sit wherever you like.

## 5. Exercises (10 questions — 5 fill-in + 5 multiple-choice)

### A. Fill-in  
1. ___ are you going? (where)  
2. ___ did you call yesterday? (who)  
3. ___ are you late? (why)  
4. ___ does this cost? (how much)  
5. ___ do you start the exam? (when)  

---

### B. Multiple-choice  
6. ___ is your favorite color?  
   a. What  
   b. Where  
   c. Who  

7. ___ do you go to school?  
   a. How  
   b. Where  
   c. When  

8. ___ are you talking to?  
   a. Who  
   b. What  
   c. How  

9. ___ is the nearest hospital?  
   a. When  
   b. Where  
   c. Which  

10. ___ did he leave so early?  
    a. Why  
    b. What  
    c. Who

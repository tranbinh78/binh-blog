# Present Perfect Continuous

## 1. Định nghĩa
Used for actions started in past and continuing now.  
Dùng cho hành động bắt đầu trong quá khứ và kéo dài đến hiện tại.

## 2. Công thức + ví dụ

### 🔸 Khẳng định
S + have/has been + V-ing  
**Example:** EN: I have been studying for 2 hours.  
VI: Tôi đã học được 2 tiếng.

### 🔸 Phủ định
S + have/has not been + V-ing  
**Example:** EN: She hasn’t been feeling well.  
VI: Cô ấy không khỏe gần đây.

### 🔸 Nghi vấn
Have/Has + S + been + V-ing?  
**Example:** EN: Have you been working?  
VI: Bạn làm việc suốt à?

## 3. Cách dùng
1. Action continuing → I’ve been reading.  
2. Action with duration → for, since.  
3. Recently finished actions → tired, sweaty.

## 4. Signals
for, since, all day, recently, lately

## 5. Bài tập
1. I ___ for 2 hours. (**study**)  
2. She ___ well. (**not/feel**)  
3. ___ you ___ long? (**work**)  
4. They ___ TV since 7. (**watch**)  
5. He ___ there. (**stay**)  

6. She ____ for hours.  
a. has worked b. has been working c. have been working d. worked  
7. I ____ since morning.  
a. have waited b. have been waiting c. waited d. waiting  
8. ____ you ____?  
a. Have/work b. Have/been working c. Has/been working d. Are/working  
9. They ____ TV.  
a. have watched b. have been watching c. watched d. watching  
10. He ____ here for long.  
a. has stayed b. has been staying c. stayed d. stays

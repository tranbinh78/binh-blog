Thì Tương Lai Hoàn Thành Tiếp Diễn (Future Perfect Continuous)

Dùng để diễn tả hành động sẽ diễn ra liên tục đến một thời điểm trong tương lai.

Công thức

S + will have been + V-ing

Ví dụ

  • I will have been studying for 3 hours by 8 PM.
    (Tôi sẽ học liên tục 3 tiếng đến 8 giờ tối.)
  • She will have been working here for 5 years.
    (Cô ấy sẽ làm việc ở đây được 5 năm.)

Bài tập

  1. I ______ (study) for 2 hours.
  2. She ______ (work) for a long time.
  3. They ______ (run) by then.
  4. We ______ (wait) for him.
  5. He ______ (not/sleep).
  6. You ______ (cook)?
  7. The kids ______ (play).
  8. My dad ______ (drive).
  9. She ______ (write).
  10. I ______ (read).

Đáp án

  1. will have been studying
  2. will have been working
  3. will have been running
  4. will have been waiting
  5. will not have been sleeping
  6. Will you have been cooking
  7. will have been playing
  8. will have been driving
  9. will have been writing
  10. will have been reading

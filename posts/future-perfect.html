Future Perfect – Thì Tương Lai Hoàn Thành

Thì Tương Lai Hoàn Thành dùng để diễn tả hành động sẽ hoàn tất trước một thời điểm trong tương lai.

1. Công thức

  • Khẳng định: S + will have + V3/ed
  • Phủ định: S + will not have + V3/ed
  • Nghi vấn: Will + S + have + V3/ed?

2. Dấu hiệu nhận biết

  • By + thời điểm tương lai (by tomorrow, by next year…)
  • Before + mốc thời gian trong tương lai

3. Ví dụ

– By next month, I will have finished the course.
– She won’t have completed the report by Monday.
– Will they have arrived by 9 PM?

4. Bài tập – Chọn dạng đúng (10 câu)

1. By next week, she ______ (finish) the project.

Đáp án: will have finished

2. They ______ (leave) before you arrive.

Đáp án: will have left

3. I ______ (complete) the assignment by tomorrow.

Đáp án: will have completed

4. She ______ (not/graduate) by next year.

Đáp án: will not have graduated

5. We ______ (arrive) by 6 PM.

Đáp án: will have arrived

6. He ______ (finish) work before the party starts.

Đáp án: will have finished

7. You ______ (read) the book by next month.

Đáp án: will have read

8. They ______ (build) the house by 2026.

Đáp án: will have built

9. She ______ (not/return) before midnight.

Đáp án: will not have returned

10. The team ______ (win) the match by then.

Đáp án: will have won

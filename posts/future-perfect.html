Thì Tương Lai Hoàn Thành (Future Perfect)

Dùng để diễn tả hành động sẽ hoàn thành trước một mốc thời gian trong tương lai.

Công thức

S + will have + V3

Ví dụ

  • I will have finished by 8 PM.
    (Tôi sẽ hoàn thành trước 8 giờ.)
  • She will have left by tomorrow.
    (Cô ấy sẽ rời đi trước ngày mai.)

Bài tập

  1. I ______ (finish) by 9 PM.
  2. She ______ (leave).
  3. They ______ (eat) already.
  4. We ______ (study) by then.
  5. He ______ (call).
  6. You ______ (arrive)?
  7. The kids ______ (sleep).
  8. My mom ______ (cook).
  9. They ______ (travel).
  10. I ______ (write).

Đáp án

  1. will have finished
  2. will have left
  3. will have eaten
  4. will have studied
  5. will have called
  6. Will you have arrived
  7. will have slept
  8. will have cooked
  9. will have traveled
  10. will have written

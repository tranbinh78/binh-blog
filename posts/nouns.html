Nouns – Danh từ

Danh từ (noun) là từ dùng để gọi tên người, vật, địa điểm, ý tưởng,…

1. Các loại danh từ

  • Danh từ chung: teacher, city, dog…
  • Danh từ riêng: John, Vietnam, Paris…
  • Danh từ đếm được: book, apple…
  • Danh từ không đếm được: water, rice, money…

2. Quy tắc thêm “s/es”

  • book → books
  • class → classes
  • baby → babies

3. Bài tập – Điền dạng đúng của danh từ (15 câu)

1. She has two _______ (child).

children

2. The _______ (city) are very crowded.

cities

3. I need some _______ (water).

(uncountable — water)

4. They bought three _______ (book).

books

5. My _______ (foot) hurts.

feet

6. The _______ (baby) is crying.

baby

7. How many _______ (person) are there?

people

8. We visited many _______ (country).

countries

9. The _______ (dish) are clean.

dishes

10. There is little _______ (milk) left.

(uncountable — milk)

11. These _______ (woman) are teachers.

women

12. I saw two _______ (mouse) in the kitchen.

mice

13. She has long _______ (tooth).

teeth

14. The _______ (bus) is full.

bus

15. They have five _______ (car).

cars

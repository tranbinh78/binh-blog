Past Perfect Continuous – Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành Tiếp Diễn

Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành Tiếp Diễn dùng để diễn tả một hành động đã diễn ra liên tục trước một thời điểm hoặc hành động khác trong quá khứ.

1. Công thức

Khẳng định: S + had been + V-ing

Phủ định: S + hadn’t been + V-ing

Nghi vấn: Had + S + been + V-ing?

2. Dấu hiệu nhận biết

  • for + khoảng thời gian
  • before + mốc thời gian
  • when + quá khứ đơn
  • until + quá khứ đơn

3. Ví dụ

– They had been waiting for two hours before the bus arrived.
– She had been studying English when I saw her.
– He hadn't been sleeping well before he went to the doctor.

4. Bài tập – Chọn dạng đúng (10 câu)

1. They ______ (wait) for an hour before the train came.

Đáp án: had been waiting

2. She ______ (study) all night before the test.

Đáp án: had been studying

3. I ______ (not/sleep) well before I visited the doctor.

Đáp án: hadn’t been sleeping

4. We ______ (work) for 3 hours when it started to rain.

Đáp án: had been working

5. He ______ (look) for his keys before he found them.

Đáp án: had been looking

6. They ______ (argue) before the teacher arrived.

Đáp án: had been arguing

7. She ______ (cry) before you called her.

Đáp án: had been crying

8. We ______ (wait) until he appeared.

Đáp án: had been waiting

9. My mother ______ (cook) for two hours before the guests arrived.

Đáp án: had been cooking

10. The kids ______ (play) until their mom picked them up.

Đáp án: had been playing

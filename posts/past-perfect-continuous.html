Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành Tiếp Diễn (Past Perfect Continuous)

Dùng để diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra liên tục trước một hành động khác trong quá khứ.

Khi nào dùng?

  • Hành động kéo dài trước một mốc thời gian quá khứ.
  • Nhấn mạnh sự liên tục.

Công thức

S + had + been + V-ing

Ví dụ

  • I had been studying for 2 hours before he arrived.
    (Tôi đã học được 2 tiếng trước khi anh ấy đến.)
  • She had been crying before I came.
    (Cô ấy đã khóc trước khi tôi đến.)

Bài tập

  1. I ______ (study) before you arrived.
  2. She ______ (cry) all morning.
  3. They ______ (run) before the rain started.
  4. We ______ (wait) for an hour.
  5. He ______ (not/work).
  6. The kids ______ (play) before dinner.
  7. You ______ (help) him?
  8. My father ______ (drive).
  9. I ______ (cook) before she came.
  10. The dog ______ (bark).

Đáp án

  1. had been studying
  2. had been crying
  3. had been running
  4. had been waiting
  5. hadn't been working
  6. had been playing
  7. Had you been helping
  8. had been driving
  9. had been cooking
  10. had been barking

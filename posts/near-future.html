1. Khái niệm

Be going to dùng để diễn tả kế hoạch đã dự định từ trước hoặc điều chắc chắn sắp xảy ra dựa trên dấu hiệu hiện tại.

2. Công thức

  • Khẳng định: S + am/is/are + going to + V
  • Phủ định: S + am/is/are + not + going to + V
  • Nghi vấn: Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V?

3. Dấu hiệu nhận biết

  • tomorrow, next week
  • soon, in a few minutes
  • Dấu hiệu rõ ràng trong hiện tại: “Look!”, “Watch out!”

4. Bài tập

  1. I ______ (visit) my grandparents tomorrow.
  2. She ______ (not/buy) that dress.
  3. They ______ (travel) to Hanoi next week.
  4. Look! The dog ______ (jump) into the water.
  5. We ______ (eat) out tonight.
  6. He ______ (not/go) to school today.
  7. You ______ (help) me later?
  8. The boys ______ (play) football soon.
  9. It ______ (rain) very soon.
  10. My mom ______ (cook) dinner.

Đáp án

1. am going to visit

2. is not going to buy

3. are going to travel

4. is going to jump

5. are going to eat

6. is not going to go

7. are going to help

8. are going to play

9. is going to rain

10. is going to cook

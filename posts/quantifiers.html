Quantifiers – Định lượng từ

Quantifiers là những từ chỉ số lượng: many, much, a few, few, a little, little, enough, too many, too much…

1. Much / Many

  • Much: dùng với danh từ không đếm được.
  • Many: dùng với danh từ đếm được số nhiều.
✓ I don’t have much money.
✓ There are many students here.

2. A little / Little – A few / Few

  • A little: một chút (không đếm được).
  • Little: rất ít, gần như không.
  • A few: một vài (đếm được).
  • Few: rất ít, gần như không.
✓ We have a few ideas.
✓ He has few friends.

3. Too much / Too many

✓ She ate too much sugar.
✓ There are too many cars on the road.

4. Enough

✓ We have enough chairs.

📝 Bài tập (10 câu)

Chọn đáp án đúng:

1. There are ___ apples in the basket.

→ many

2. She drank ___ water today.

→ too much

3. We have ___ time left, so hurry up!

→ little

4. He has ___ friends — maybe only two.

→ few

5. I need ___ money to buy the book.

→ a little

6. There are ___ students in the class — almost 40!

→ too many

7. Do we have ___ chairs for everyone?

→ enough

8. They made ___ mistakes in the test.

→ many

9. She put ___ salt in the soup. It's too salty.

→ too much

10. We have ___ options — we must decide soon.

→ few

Trạng từ là gì?

Trạng từ (adverb) là từ dùng để bổ nghĩa cho động từ, tính từ hoặc cả câu.

Ví dụ:
– She sings beautifully.
– He is very tall.
– Luckily, he passed the exam.

Bài tập – Chọn trạng từ đúng (15 câu)

1. She sings very ______. (beautiful)

Đáp án: beautifully

2. He runs ______ than me. (fast)

Đáp án: faster

3. They speak English very ______. (good)

Đáp án: well

4. The test was ______ easy. (surprising)

Đáp án: surprisingly

5. She arrived ______ for the meeting. (late)

Đáp án: late

6. He drives too ______. (dangerous)

Đáp án: dangerously

7. The baby slept ______ through the night. (quiet)

Đáp án: quietly

8. He learns very ______. (quick)

Đáp án: quickly

9. They work ______ together. (good)

Đáp án: well

10. She answered the question ______. (correct)

Đáp án: correctly

11. She smiled ______ at me. (happy)

Đáp án: happily

12. The rain fell ______. (heavy)

Đáp án: heavily

13. He shouted ______. (angry)

Đáp án: angrily

14. The dog barked ______. (loud)

Đáp án: loudly

15. They waited ______ for the bus. (patient)

Đáp án: patiently

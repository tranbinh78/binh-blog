Conjunctions – Liên từ

Liên từ là từ dùng để nối các từ, cụm từ hoặc mệnh đề trong câu.

3 loại chính của liên từ

1. Coordinating Conjunctions (Liên từ kết hợp)

for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so — viết tắt FANBOYS

Ví dụ:
– I wanted to go out, but it was raining.
– She is smart and hardworking.

2. Subordinating Conjunctions (Liên từ phụ thuộc)

because, although, while, if, when, since, unless …

Ví dụ:
– I stayed home because I was tired.
– Although it rained, we continued the trip.

3. Correlative Conjunctions (Liên từ tương quan)

either… or / neither… nor / not only… but also / both… and

Ví dụ:
– Either you study hard, or you will fail.
– She is both beautiful and smart.

Bài tập

Chọn liên từ thích hợp để hoàn thành câu.

  1. I was tired, ___ I went to bed early.
  2. She wanted to go out ___ it was raining.
  3. He stayed home ___ he was sick.
  4. We will start ___ everyone is ready.
  5. ___ it was cold, we went swimming.
  6. You can take a taxi ___ you can walk.
  7. She is smart ___ hardworking.
  8. I will call you ___ I arrive.
  9. He passed the exam ___ he worked very hard.
  10. Either you apologize ___ you leave.
